# Sage (Retell) — Knowledge Questions for Belinda

The questions below are written **for the voice agent.** Belinda's answers become the knowledge base we load into Retell — the front-desk agent answers callers *only* from this. Keep answers short and spoken-style (it's read aloud on a call).

> Paste the filled answers into **Front Desk → Knowledge base** (upload or paste). Anything left blank, Sage simply won't claim to know.

---

## 1. How Sage answers the phone
- **1.1** Word-for-word, how should Sage greet a caller? *(e.g. "Thanks for calling The Green Room Beauty Bar, this is Sage — how can I help?")*
- **1.2** Should Sage say it's an AI assistant, or just act as "the front desk"?
- **1.3** Three words for the tone on the phone (warm? quick? polished?).
- **1.4** How should it close a call? *(e.g. "Anything else? — Great, see you soon.")*

## 2. The essentials callers ask first
- **2.1** Open hours by day (and any days fully closed).
- **2.2** Address + the one-line "how to find us / where to park."
- **2.3** Is the salon open holidays? Which ones closed?
- **2.4** Phone/text-back number callers should use, and your website/booking link.

## 3. Services & how to talk about price
- **3.1** Main services offered across the salon (cuts, color, balayage, barber, brows, extensions…).
- **3.2** Roughly how long do the top services take? *(so Sage can set expectations)*
- **3.3** When asked "how much is ___," what should Sage do — give a range, or say "your stylist confirms pricing"? **Pick one.**
- **3.4** If ranges are OK: rough range for your 5–8 most-asked services.
- **3.5** Services you do **not** offer (so Sage never promises them).
- **3.6** Which services need a consultation first (big color changes, extensions)?

## 4. Booking — the main job
- **4.1** Should Sage **book the appointment**, or **take the request and the stylist confirms**? **Pick one** *(we recommend route-and-confirm for V1).*
- **4.2** What does Sage need from a caller to book/route? *(name, phone, service, which stylist, preferred day/time)*
- **4.3** Do you take walk-ins? Under what conditions?
- **4.4** How far ahead do you book? Any minimum notice?
- **4.5** Reschedules and cancellations — how should Sage handle each?

## 5. Who's who — routing
- **5.1** Roster, one line each: **Name — what they do — taking new clients? — how to book them.**
- **5.2** If a caller doesn't know who to ask for, how should Sage help them choose? *(by service / availability / price)*
- **5.3** If the requested stylist is booked or off, what's the move? *(waitlist, suggest someone, take a message)*
- **5.4** Anyone Sage should **never** book for, or should always hand to a human?

## 6. Policies Sage should state
- **6.1** Cancellation policy — notice required + any fee.
- **6.2** No-show policy.
- **6.3** Late arrival — grace period before it's a reschedule.
- **6.4** Deposits — which services, how much.
- **6.5** Kids / extra guests / pets in the salon — allowed?
- **6.6** Patch-test / allergy rule for color or chemical services.

## 7. Retail & products *(this switches on Sage's retail ability)*
- **7.1** What product brands/lines do you carry and recommend? *(e.g. Oribe, Olaplex, K18…)*
- **7.2** Can clients buy product without an appointment? In-store only, or can you ship/hold?
- **7.3** Gift cards — sold? How are they bought and redeemed?
- **7.4** When should Sage *suggest* a product or gift card vs. stay hands-off? *(e.g. only if asked, or gently offer at booking)*
- **7.5** Anything product-related Sage should never do *(quote exact prices, take card numbers over the phone, etc.)*.

> If §7 is left blank, Sage's retail answers stay **off** — it won't recommend or sell. Fill it in to switch the capability on.

## 8. Frequent questions (highest value — spend the most time here)
- **8.1** List the 10–20 questions you get asked most.
- **8.2** For each, the one-line answer you'd want Sage to give.
- **8.3** Any question that today gets the *wrong* answer / causes confusion?

## 9. After-hours & handing off to a human
- **9.1** What should happen to calls that come in after hours or when everyone's busy? *(take a message, offer text-back, send booking link)*
- **9.2** Who does Sage escalate to, and for what? *(Belinda for client issues, Joe for logistics, etc.)*
- **9.3** What's worth interrupting you for vs. "handle in the morning"?
- **9.4** Response time Sage can promise *("we'll text you back within ___")*.

## 10. Persona & hard boundaries
- **10.1** Words/phrases you want Sage to use. Words it must avoid.
- **10.2** How should it handle an upset or rude caller — apologize + take a message, or transfer?
- **10.3** Sales calls / spam / vendors — what should it do?
- **10.4** The hard "never" list: things Sage must **never** say or do on your behalf *(promise refunds, give medical/allergy advice, quote firm prices, speak for a stylist, share another stylist's numbers)*.
- **10.5** Languages Sage should handle.

---

### How this becomes Sage's brain
1. Belinda fills these in (or sends a doc).
2. Paste/upload into **Front Desk → Knowledge base**.
3. Save → in live mode it syncs to the Retell agent as Sage's knowledge.
4. Filling §7 flips the **Retail** capability on automatically.
