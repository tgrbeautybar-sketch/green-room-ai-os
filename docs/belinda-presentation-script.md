# Walkthrough with Belinda — Presentation Script

Plain-language talk track. No tech terms. Explanations + what you need from her.

---

## 1. The big picture (30 sec)
"Belinda, what we've built for you is basically a small team working in the background — an AI receptionist named **Sage** who answers your phone, and a helper that **writes and posts your social media** in your voice. The goal is to take the phone tag, the missed calls, and the hours on Instagram off your plate — *without changing how your salon runs.*"

## 2. Sage — your AI receptionist
"Sage answers your salon's calls. She already knows your hours, services, stylists, and policies — from the answers you sent me. When someone calls:
- **If they want to book** — she takes their name, number, what they want, and who they'd like to see, and sends it straight to you.
- **If they need a real person** — she transfers the call right to you.
- **If it's after hours** — she takes a message and emails it to you, so nothing slips through."

**How you keep your number:** "You keep your number *exactly* as it is — nothing changes for you or your texts. Sage gets her own dedicated line that becomes your main front-desk number, and any call that needs you, she passes straight to your phone."

👉 **What I need from you:** a yes to set up Sage's line, the email where she should send you messages, and a few quick clarifications (next).

## 3. A few things to make Sage accurate
"Just a little more so she never gives a wrong answer:
1. Every stylist sets their own hours — should Sage say a general 'we're open Tuesday–Saturday,' or always 'hours depend on your stylist, let me get you to the right one'?
2. Which stylists are taking **new clients** right now?
3. What are **Jillian** and **Charisse** known for?
4. Who does the **massage, facials, waxing, and nails**? (Your list was hair + makeup.)
5. When someone needs a real person, is that always **you**, or someone else?
6. No parking out front — any **nearby spot** you'd want her to point people to?"

## 4. Post Studio — your social, handled
"This writes your Instagram and Facebook posts in your salon's voice — brand posts, 'chair available' posts — you just **review and approve**, and it posts for you. No more staring at a blank caption box at 9pm."

👉 **What I need from you:** your Instagram needs to be a free **Business or Creator** account connected to your **Facebook page** — that's the only way *any* tool is allowed to post for you. If it's a personal account, it's a free 2-minute switch and I'll walk you through it on a quick screen-share.

## 5. Your numbers dashboard (comes a little after)
"This is the one screen that shows your real numbers — what you're bringing in, your busiest services, your rent collected — so you can answer your own questions without a bookkeeper. It connects to your booking system and your bank, and those approvals take a few business days, so it lands a little **after** the front desk and social. But it's coming."

## 6. Friday rent
"The system writes your Friday rent reminders for you, in your tone — for now you just hit **send** from your phone. A little later we can make those go out **fully automatically**. Either way, no more chasing it in your head."

## 7. Your private login
"You'll have your own private login to see everything in one place — your messages, your posts, your numbers."

## 8. Honest timeline
"To be straight with you: the **front desk and social** can come together pretty quickly. The **numbers dashboard and fully-automatic texting** take a few extra days because we're waiting on approvals from your booking system, your bank, and the phone carriers — that's normal, just not same-day."

## 9. What I need from you — the short list
1. A **yes** to set up Sage's dedicated line.
2. Your **email** for messages and bookings.
3. Your **Instagram set as Business/Creator** + linked to your Facebook page (I'll guide you).
4. Answers to the **6 quick questions** above.

"That's it — everything else is on me."

## Close
"Once I have those, I'll get Sage answering and your posts going, and we'll set up the numbers piece right after. Sound good?"
