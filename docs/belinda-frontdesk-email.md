# Email to Belinda — Simple Front Desk Questions

> Plain-language intake for a *simple* front desk (Sage). No price quoting, no retail selling, no complex logic — answer the phone, share info, take a booking message, hand off when needed. Send by email; she replies in any format.

---

**Subject:** A few quick questions to set up your AI front desk

Hi Belinda,

We're setting up your AI front desk — we're calling her **Sage** — so she answers the phone the way you would. To do that, I just need you to answer a few simple questions below. Nothing technical, and there are no wrong answers — it's just how you'd want the phone handled.

Short answers or bullet points are perfect, and skip anything that doesn't apply. You can reply right here in the email.

**The basics**
1. How should Sage answer the phone? *(e.g. "Thanks for calling The Green Room, this is Sage — how can I help?")*
2. What are your hours, by day?
3. What's your address, and where do people park?

**Your services**
4. What services does the salon offer? *(Just a list is fine — cuts, color, balayage, barber, brows, etc.)*
5. When someone asks "how much is ___?", is it okay for Sage to say "your stylist will confirm the price when you book"? *(Keeps it simple — yes or no.)*

**Booking & your team**
6. When someone wants to book, Sage will take their name, number, what they want, and who they'd like to see, then pass it along to you/the stylist. Does that work, or would you prefer something different?
7. A quick list of your stylists — name, what they're known for, and whether they're taking new clients.
8. If a caller isn't sure who to book with, how should Sage help them choose?

**A few policies**
9. What's your cancellation policy? *(How much notice, and any fee?)*
10. What happens if someone's running late or doesn't show up?
11. Do you take deposits for anything?

**The questions you get all the time**
12. What are the questions you get asked most on the phone — and the short answer you'd want Sage to give? *(This is the most helpful one. Even 5–10 is great.)*

**When you're closed or busy**
13. If a call comes in after hours or when everyone's busy, what should Sage do? *(Take a message, text them back, send a booking link?)*
14. How do you want Sage to get messages to you — text, email, or WhatsApp?

**Anything off-limits**
15. Is there anything you'd want Sage to never do or say? *(For example: never quote exact prices, or never give advice about allergies — she'll just take a message instead.)*

That's everything. Reply whenever it's easy — typed out, bullet points, whatever's simplest for you. Once I have your answers, Sage will be ready to start answering your phone in your voice.

Talk soon,
[Your name]
