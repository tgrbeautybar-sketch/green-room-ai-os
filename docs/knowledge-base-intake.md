# The Green Room — AI Knowledge Base & SOP Intake

This is the brain behind your AI. Every tool — the **Front Desk Agent**, **Post Studio**, the **Dashboard**, and the **Rent Roll** — only knows what's written here. The more you fill in, the more it sounds like *you* and the fewer mistakes it makes on the phone or in a caption.

**How to fill this out**
- Answer in your own words. Short is fine. Bullet points are fine.
- If something doesn't apply, write **"N/A."** If you're not sure, write **"not sure"** — we'll fill the gaps together later.
- You don't have to do it in one sitting. Do a section, come back.
- Wherever it says *per-stylist*, that means each of the 12 chairs may answer differently — give what you know; the rest lives with each stylist.

## 1. The Basics
*Why the AI needs this: it introduces your business on every call and post.*

1.1 What's the full legal business name, and the name customers actually use?
1.2 One sentence: how would you describe The Green Room to someone who's never heard of it?
1.3 What kind of salon is it (hair, color, barber, esthetics, nails, mix)? List everything done under your roof.
1.4 How long have you been open / what year did you start?
1.5 Who owns and runs it day to day? (Names + roles — e.g. Belinda, Joe.)
1.6 What's the chair-rental model in plain words? (Are all stylists independent? Do they keep 100%? Flat rent?)
1.7 What's the main phone number callers reach? Is it one number for the whole salon, or one per stylist today?
1.8 What's the booking website / Instagram / Facebook / Google profile link?
1.9 What's the best email for general inquiries?

## 2. Location & Getting There
*Why: callers ask "where are you / where do I park" constantly.*

2.1 Full street address.
2.2 Any landmarks or "we're next to ___" directions people need?
2.3 Parking: free lot, street, garage, validation? Anything tricky?
2.4 Public transit options nearby?
2.5 Which floor / suite / how do they find the front door once they arrive?
2.6 Accessibility: wheelchair accessible? Stairs? Elevator?

## 3. Hours & Availability
*Why: the agent must never promise a time you're closed.*

3.1 What are the salon's general open hours, by day?
3.2 Are stylists' individual hours different from the building hours? (*per-stylist*)
3.3 Days you're fully closed (e.g. Sundays, Mondays)?
3.4 Holidays you close for?
3.5 Do you do early/late by appointment? Who decides — you or each stylist?
3.6 Busiest days/times (so the agent can set expectations on wait/availability)?

## 4. The Team
*Why: the agent has to route callers to the right person.*

4.1 Roster — one line per stylist is all we need here. Format:
    **Name — what they do — taking new clients? (yes/waitlist/no) — how to book them (link, "call us," "DM," etc.)**
    *(This is the only per-stylist info the front desk needs to route a call. Don't chase their prices/hours — those live with each stylist.)*
4.2 For anyone worth highlighting, what are they *known for* / their specialty?
4.3 Anyone who is full / waitlist only / referrals only?
4.4 Are there any stylists who only take returning clients or referrals?
4.5 Does anyone speak a second language clients might ask for?
4.6 How should the agent describe you (Belinda) specifically when someone asks for the owner?
4.7 If a caller doesn't know who to book with, how should the agent help them choose? (By service? By price? By availability?)
4.8 Is there anyone the agent should **not** book for / should always escalate to a human?

## 5. Services & Menu
*Why: "do you do ___ and how much" is the #1 call.*

5.1 Is there a salon-wide service menu, or does each stylist have their own list? (*per-stylist if separate*)
5.2 List the main services offered across the salon (cuts, color, balayage, extensions, barber, brows, etc.).
5.3 For each main service, roughly how long does it take?
5.4 Which services need a consultation first (e.g. big color corrections, extensions)?
5.5 Any services you specifically **do not** offer (so the agent doesn't promise them)?
5.6 Any signature/popular packages or combos worth mentioning?
5.7 Add-ons people often forget to book (toner, gloss, treatment, blowout)?
5.8 Anything seasonal (wedding/bridal, prom, holiday)?

## 6. Pricing & Payments
*Why: the agent needs rules for what it can quote.*

6.1 In a chair-rental setup, who sets prices — you or each stylist? (*per-stylist*)
6.2 When a caller asks "how much for ___," what should the agent do — give a price, give a range, or say the stylist will confirm? (Pick one default.)
6.3 If ranges are OK, give a rough range for your top 5–8 services.
6.4 What payment methods do you accept (card, cash, Venmo, Zelle, Apple Pay, Square)?
6.5 Do you take deposits for any services? Which, and how much?
6.6 Tipping — cash only, on card, suggested %? What should the agent say if asked?
6.7 Do you sell gift cards? How are they bought and redeemed?
6.8 Any memberships, loyalty, or package pricing?
6.9 First-time client discounts or referral perks?

## 7. Booking & Scheduling
*Why: this is what the agent spends most of its time doing.*

7.1 How do clients book today — online link, phone, text, DM, walk-in?
7.2 What booking systems are in use, and which stylist uses which? (Vagaro / Square / Millennium / manual) (*per-stylist*)
7.3 Should the agent actually book the appointment, or just take the request and have the stylist confirm? (Pick a default for now.)
7.4 How far in advance do you take bookings? Any minimum notice?
7.5 Do you take walk-ins? Under what conditions?
7.6 How do you handle a waitlist / cancellation list?
7.7 What info do you need from a new client to book (name, phone, service, with whom)?
7.8 New clients vs returning — anything handled differently?
7.9 How should reschedules be handled?
7.10 Confirmation + reminder messages — do you send them, when, and what do they say?

## 8. Policies
*Why: the agent must enforce these consistently so you don't have to.*

8.1 Cancellation policy — how much notice, any fee?
8.2 No-show policy — what happens, any charge?
8.3 Late arrival — grace period? When do you have to reschedule them?
8.4 Refunds / "I'm not happy with my hair" — what's your policy and redo window?
8.5 Deposit forfeiture rules?
8.6 Kids / extra guests in the salon — allowed?
8.7 Pets / service animals?
8.8 Age minimum for any services (e.g. color, chemical)?
8.9 Patch test / allergy policy for color or chemical services?
8.10 Anything else clients should know before they come in?

## 9. The Salon Experience
*Why: it helps the agent and the captions sound warm and specific.*

9.1 What's the vibe/atmosphere you want people to feel (calm, luxe, neighborhood, edgy)?
9.2 Amenities — drinks, wifi, music, kids' corner, retail products?
9.3 What products/brands do you carry or use (color lines, retail shelf)?
9.4 What do clients say they love most about coming in?
9.5 Anything that makes a first visit feel special?

## 10. Front Desk Agent ("Sage") — How It Should Behave
*Why: this defines the personality and the guardrails of the phone/text agent.*

10.1 What should the agent be named? (Default: **Sage** — keep or change.)
10.2 Should it tell callers it's an AI assistant, or just act as "the front desk"?
10.3 Tone on the phone — warm and chatty, fast and efficient, polished and formal? Give 2–3 words.
10.4 Write (or describe) the **greeting** you'd want it to open with.
10.5 What is the agent **allowed** to do on its own? (book, reschedule, cancel, quote prices, give hours/address, take messages)
10.6 What must it **never** do without a human? (give medical/allergy advice, promise refunds, quote firm prices, discuss another stylist's business)
10.7 When someone is upset or complaining, what should it do — apologize and take a message, or transfer to you/Joe?
10.8 If a caller asks for a specific stylist who's busy/booked, what's the move?
10.9 If the agent doesn't know an answer, what should it say and do?
10.10 Any words, phrases, or slang you want it to use? Any it must avoid?
10.11 Should it collect phone numbers / consent to text reminders? How should it ask?
10.12 Languages it should handle?

## 11. After-Hours & Escalation
*Why: most missed leads happen when no one's at the desk.*

11.1 What should happen to calls/texts that come in after hours or when everyone's with a client?
11.2 Who is the human escalation contact, and for what? (e.g. Joe for rent/logistics, Belinda for client issues.)
11.3 What's an "emergency" worth interrupting you for vs. "handle in the morning"?
11.4 How and where do you want new booking requests / messages delivered to you? (Text, WhatsApp, email, dashboard?)
11.5 Expected response time you want to promise callers ("we'll get back to you within ___").

## 12. Brand Voice & Social (Post Studio)
*Why: this is what every caption is written from.*

12.1 Describe your brand voice in 3–5 adjectives.
12.2 Paste 2–3 captions you've posted that felt *exactly* right.
12.3 Paste 1–2 captions/posts (yours or others') that are **not** your style.
12.4 Words, phrases, or emojis you love using.
12.5 Words, emojis, or topics that are **off-limits** (no clickbait, no discounting language, etc.)?
12.6 What do you post about — content pillars? (e.g. transformations, the team, the space, openings, education, behind-the-scenes)
12.7 How often do you want to post, and on which platforms (IG, FB, both)?
12.8 Best days/times you like to post?
12.9 What's the usual call-to-action ("book at the link in bio," "DM us," "tour this week")?
12.10 Default hashtag set you want on most posts?
12.11 Can the salon page feature individual stylists' work, or is the page strictly the salon brand?
12.12 Who approves a post before it goes out — just you, or anyone else?
12.13 Are there promotions/sales you run, and how should those be worded?
12.14 Tagging — do you tag stylists, products, location? Any rules?

## 13. Money & Metrics (Dashboard)
*Why: this decides which numbers the dashboard shows you and what "a good week" means.*

13.1 As the owner, what makes up **your** income — your own services, chair rent, retail, all of it? (Be specific so we don't mix your money with the stylists'.)
13.2 What questions do you wish you could answer at a glance every week? (List them — these become the dashboard.)
13.3 What's a *good* week in dollars? A *concerning* week?
13.4 Roughly what does product/color cost you as a % of a service (so "net" is accurate)?
13.5 What's a healthy chair occupancy in your eyes (e.g. booked 4+/day)?
13.6 How do you define a "retained" client (came back within how many weeks)?
13.7 Do you want to see the whole room's performance, just your chair, or both?
13.8 Any number you currently track in your head or on paper that we should put on screen?
13.9 What report would you want emailed/texted to you, and how often?

## 14. Chair Rental Operations (Rent Roll)
*Why: this runs the Friday rent reminders and chase-ups for you.*

14.1 What does rent cost per chair/suite type? (*per-stylist or by chair type*)
14.2 What day is rent due, and is there a grace period?
14.3 How is rent paid (Venmo, Zelle, cash, Square)? Any preferred method?
14.4 Is there a late fee? How much / when does it kick in?
14.5 What's included in rent (suite, color bar, laundry, towels, parking, marketing)?
14.6 What is **not** included that stylists pay for themselves?
14.7 New stylist onboarding — deposit, first/last month, agreement, notice to move in?
14.8 Notice period for a stylist who wants to leave?
14.9 What tone should rent reminders have — friendly, neutral, firm?
14.10 If someone hasn't paid by ___, what should happen and who handles it (you or Joe)?
14.11 Anything about rent you'd rather a human always handle, not the AI?

## 15. Common Caller Questions (FAQ)
*Why: feed it the real questions and it answers instantly, correctly.*

15.1 List the 10–20 questions you and your team get asked most often.
15.2 For each, the short answer you'd want given. *(One line each is fine — this is the highest-value section, spend the most time here.)*
15.3 Any question that always gets the wrong answer / causes confusion today?

## 16. Edge Cases, Complaints & "Never Do This"
*Why: this keeps the AI from embarrassing you.*

16.1 How should a refund or "redo my hair" request be handled, start to finish?
16.2 How should an angry or rude caller be handled?
16.3 What should it do with sales calls, spam, or vendors?
16.4 Press/media or partnership requests — who gets those?
16.5 If someone asks about a stylist who no longer works there, what's the response?
16.6 If someone asks for medical, allergy, or "is this safe for me" advice — what's the boundary?
16.7 List anything the AI should **absolutely never say or do** on your behalf.
16.8 Any topics that are sensitive for your salon specifically?

## 17. Competitors & What Makes You Different
*Why: helps positioning in captions and on calls.*

17.1 Who are your main local competitors?
17.2 Why do clients choose you over them?
17.3 What do you do that nobody else nearby does?
17.4 Any salons/accounts whose style or marketing you admire?

## 18. Safety & Compliance
*Why: keeps you protected and the AI honest.*

18.1 Licensing — are all stylists independently licensed and insured? Anything the front desk should confirm/never promise?
18.2 Patch test / consultation requirements for chemical services?
18.3 Allergy or sensitivity intake — do you collect this, and how?
18.4 Any health/sanitation policies clients ask about?
18.5 Cancellation/medical exceptions you make case-by-case?
18.6 Anything legal or compliance-related the AI must route to a human?
